The water cycle explains how water moves continuously between the Earth and the atmosphere!
Heat from the sun makes the water in oceans, lakes and rivers evaporate and rise as vapour.
When the vapour rises it cools down and condenses into tiny droplets that form clouds.
Once the droplets join together and become heavy, they fall back to the ground as rain, snow or hail;
this step is called precipitation. Part of the water flows over the land as runoff and goes back to the oceans,
and part of it soaks into the soil and becomes groundwater. The water cycle is essential for life on our planet.
